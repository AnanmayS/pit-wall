# Results

Every number here was produced by the command shown next to it, on this machine, on the date given.
Nothing is estimated. "Synthetic" marks a workload whose shape was chosen by us; the measurement is
still real. Items marked **pending** are waiting on data, not on code.

Machine: Apple Silicon Mac (Darwin 25.3), Docker Desktop, ClickHouse 25.8 and DynamoDB Local in
containers, Python 3.12, uvicorn single process. Measured 2026-10-06/07.

Data state at measurement: all **60** 2025 R/Q/S/SQ sessions ingested (the first pass loaded 45 before
FastF1's 500-calls/hour limit; the rest were loaded by the resumable retry script).

### Pre-race win probability, ours vs Polymarket (2025, Brier score, lower is better)

Our forecast for round r uses only races 1..r-1 (recent-form fit, no qualifying model). The market
price is sampled at a stated time before the race start.

| Market sampled | Races | Our Brier | Market Brier | Our log-loss | Market log-loss | Races we beat the market | Command |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 h before the race (market knows the grid) | 18 | 0.844 | 0.524 | 1.805 | 0.973 | 5/18 | `uv run pitwall odds score --season 2025 --sims 5000 --hours-before 1` |
| 48 h before the race (before qualifying) | 18 | 0.844 | 0.781 | 1.805 | 1.653 | 6/18 | `uv run pitwall odds score --season 2025 --sims 5000 --hours-before 48` |

Reading: the market is better, by a wide margin once it has seen qualifying and by a small margin
before. The gap before qualifying is the honest size of what the recent-form model is missing
(track-specific pace, upgrades, weather). Per-race rows: `data/bench/odds_score_{1h,48h}.json`.
